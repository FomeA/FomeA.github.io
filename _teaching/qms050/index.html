Introduction

Course overview and purpose.

This course aims equipping candidates with the necessary knowledge and skills of identifying techniques in presenting, summarizing and analysing data. It also enables students to apply mathematical and statistical tools in business and planning process.

Learning outcomes

At the end of the course, each student should be able to:

1 Solve business problems using mathematical and statistical techniques.
2 Apply mathematical and statistical knowledge in presenting, summarizing and analysing data.

Teaching methodology

Approaches used throughout the course.

Teaching approaches Question and answer, group discussion, and interactive lecture.
