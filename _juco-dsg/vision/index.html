Data Science & Mathematical Modelling Research Group

A research initiative dedicated to advancing analytical thinking, statistical modelling, and evidence-based solutions for society.

Our Vision

To become a leading centre of excellence in data science and mathematical modelling, producing impactful research that transforms decision-making in Tanzania and beyond.

Our Mission

To train researchers in data science and applied mathematics, foster innovation in analytical methods, and deliver research-driven solutions to real-world challenges.

Core Values

These principles guide how we think, work, and collaborate every day.

Excellence
We uphold the highest standards in research, analysis, and academic output through continuous improvement.
Integrity
We act with honesty, transparency, and strong ethical responsibility in all research activities.
Innovation
We embrace creative thinking and modern analytical tools to solve real-world problems.
Collaboration
We work across disciplines and share knowledge to strengthen collective research impact.
Empowerment
We equip early-career researchers with skills, confidence, and meaningful research experience.
Impact
We produce research that directly informs decisions and improves society.
