Who We Are

JUCo DSRG is an interdisciplinary research initiative focused on data science, statistical analysis, and mathematical modelling.

We train researchers, develop analytical capacity, and generate research that supports evidence-based decision making for Tanzania and beyond.

Participants work collaboratively, receive mentorship, learn modern analytical tools, and contribute to meaningful research addressing institutional, health, and development challenges.

Learn more about our vision →
```
```

Research Areas

Our research applies quantitative methods to practical institutional, health, and development challenges.

Institutional Modelling

Planning, resource allocation, educational quality, and sustainable growth within higher education systems.

Health & Wellbeing Modelling

Quantitative approaches to public health, resilience, wellbeing, and equitable access to services.

What You Will Gain

The programme combines technical training, research experience, mentorship, and academic development.

Data Science Skills

Hands-on R and Python programming, data analysis, and visualization.

Mathematical Modelling

Research Experience

Participate in genuine research projects with practical impact.

Academic Development

Mentorship

Career Preparation

Who Can Join?

Eligibility

  • Mathematics undergraduates
  • Computer Science undergraduates
  • Postgraduate researchers
  • At least two academic years remaining for undergraduates

Selection Criteria

  • Strong motivation for research
  • Commitment to weekly participation
  • Interest in quantitative methods
  • No prior coding experience required
