Who We Are

JUCo DSRG is an interdisciplinary research initiative focused on data science, statistical analysis, and mathematical modelling. We train researchers, develop analytical capacity, and generate research that supports evidence-based decision making for Tanzania and beyond.

As a supportive research community, we help bridge the gap between classroom learning and real-world analytical practice. Participants work in collaborative teams, receive mentorship, learn modern analytical tools, and contribute to meaningful research projects addressing institutional, health, and development challenges.


Learn More →

Research Areas

The JUCo DSRG conducts interdisciplinary research aligned with global development priorities, evidence-based policymaking, and data-driven institutional strengthening.


Institutional Modelling focuses on planning, resource allocation, educational quality, and sustainable growth within higher education systems.


Health and Wellbeing Modelling focuses on public health, resilience, wellbeing, and equitable access to services through quantitative research and analytical methods.

Who Can Join?

Eligibility

  • Mathematics undergraduates
  • Computer Science undergraduates
  • Postgraduate researchers
  • At least two academic years remaining (undergraduates)

Selection Criteria

  • Strong motivation for research
  • Commitment to weekly participation
  • Interest in quantitative methods
  • No prior coding experience required
