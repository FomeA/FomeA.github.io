About the Group

Research at the intersection of mathematics, data and society.

The JUCo Data Science & Modelling Research Group is an interdisciplinary initiative designed to introduce early-career researchers to quantitative research, statistical analysis, computational methods, and mathematical modelling.

The group provides a collaborative environment where students and researchers can develop practical research skills while working on problems relevant to higher education, health, wellbeing, and sustainable development.

Learn more about our research vision and the direction of the group →

Learn. Model. Analyse. Research. Building quantitative research capacity through interdisciplinary collaboration.
Research Directions

Problems worth modelling.

The group applies quantitative and computational approaches to practical institutional and societal challenges.

01

Institutional Modelling

Quantitative approaches to planning, resource allocation, educational quality, decision-making, and sustainable growth in higher education institutions.

02

Health & Wellbeing Modelling

Data-driven research addressing public health, resilience, wellbeing, and equitable access through statistical analysis, mathematical modelling, and analytical methods.

Who Can Join

Open to emerging researchers.

The programme is designed for students and researchers interested in quantitative and interdisciplinary research.

  • Mathematics undergraduate students
  • Computer Science undergraduate students
  • Postgraduate researchers
  • Undergraduates with at least two academic years remaining
  • Applicants interested in quantitative research methods
Women are encouraged to apply. We particularly welcome applications from women interested in mathematics, data science, statistics, computing, and quantitative research.
