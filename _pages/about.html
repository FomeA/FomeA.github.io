Research

Understanding complex systems through mathematics and data.

My research combines mathematical modelling, statistical analysis, and computational methods to investigate practical problems in infectious diseases and public health.

Mathematical Modelling of Infectious Diseases

I develop and analyse quantitative models to understand disease dynamics, interactions between infections, and factors that influence public-health outcomes. My work brings together mathematical modelling, biostatistics, numerical methods, and statistical computing.

Malaria & TB

Quantitative approaches to malaria and tuberculosis co-infections.

TB & HIV

Modelling co-infection dynamics and implications for public health.

Respiratory Diseases

Competitive disease systems, quarantine, and cross-immunity.

Statistical & Computational Methods

Data-driven analysis supporting mathematical and applied research.

From theory to evidence

The goal is not only to develop mathematical theory, but to use quantitative methods to generate evidence that can help explain complex systems and inform decision-making.
