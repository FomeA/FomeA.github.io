About Me

I am a researcher in Applied Mathematics and Statistics at Jordan University College, Tanzania. My work integrates mathematical modelling, biostatistics and data analysis to address problems in health, education and sustainable development.

I collaborate across disciplines to produce evidence that informs policy, improves decision-making and strengthens research capacity.
