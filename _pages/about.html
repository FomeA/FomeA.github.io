Research Philosophy

From mathematical ideas to practical impact.

I am interested in research that does more than produce numbers. The goal is to develop quantitative approaches that help explain real problems, communicate evidence clearly, and support better decisions.

My interests particularly focus on applications relevant to Tanzania and other settings where rigorous quantitative evidence can contribute to sustainable development.
