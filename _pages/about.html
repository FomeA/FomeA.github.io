Welcome to my academic website. My research explores how applied mathematics, statistics, and data science can address practical challenges in public health, education, and community development.

I am particularly interested in mathematical modelling, biostatistics, epidemiology, and evidence-based decision-making that supports sustainable development across Tanzania and beyond.
