JUCo Data Science & Modelling Research Group (JUCo DSRG)

An Interdisciplinary Early-Career Research Initiative · Jordan University College, Morogoro

JUCo DSRG is a new, formally established research initiative at Jordan University College. We are looking for motivated early-career researchers — undergraduate students and postgraduate researchers — to join our first cohort and help us build something genuinely new at JUCo: a research environment where data analysis, mathematical modelling, and real-world problem-solving come together.

Apply Now Explore Programme

Programme Overview

The JUCo DSG is a supportive, 12-month pilot research community designed to bridge the gap between classroom theory and practical, real-world skills. Working together in small, collaborative teams, participants will receive dedicated mentorship to analyze data, build models, and develop impactful research projects that address critical health, education, and development challenges in our community.

Training Curriculum (2026/2027 Cohort)

R Programming & Applied Data Analysis Foundations of Mathematical Modelling Individual Research Project Development Internal Scientific Seminar & Conference Transition to Formal Research Projects

Who Can Apply (Pilot Year)

Women in STEM: We strongly encourage female students to apply. We provide a supportive environment with peer mentorship to help you thrive!
  • Open to Mathematics or Computer Science undergraduates.
  • Open to Postgraduate Research Scientists.
  • Must have at least 2 academic years remaining (for undergraduates).

Selection Criteria

  • Strong motivation for research and learning.
  • Commitment to engaging in weekly sessions.
  • An interest in quantitative methods.
  • No prior coding experience required — we teach you from scratch!

Commitment to Growth: We welcome students of all backgrounds who meet the eligibility criteria above. If you bring the dedication to learn, we will provide the tools for you to succeed.

Expected Impact

Empowered students with advanced, job-ready analytical skills
A thriving, supportive research culture and mentorship network
Data-driven solutions for real-world institutional and health challenges
Building the foundation for a premier Centre of Excellence
