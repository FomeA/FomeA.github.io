What is JUCo DSG?

JUCo Data Science & Modelling Research Group is a hands-on research training environment where students work on real-world datasets and develop analytical thinking through guided research projects.

What You Will Gain

💻 Coding in R (from basics to applied analysis)
📊 Real-world data analysis experience
🧠 Mathematical modelling skills
📈 Research project for your CV
🎤 Presentation & communication skills
🎓 Preparation for postgraduate studies
🌍 Problem-solving for real African challenges
🏅 Certificate of participation

Who Can Apply?

  • Mathematics or Computer Science students
  • Postgraduate research students
  • At least 2 years remaining (undergraduates)
  • No prior programming experience required

Programme Structure

  • Weekly guided sessions (2 hours)
  • Project-based learning approach
  • Group discussions & mentorship
  • Individual research development

How To Join
