CALL FOR PARTICIPANTS

JUCo Data Science & Modelling Research Group

An Interdisciplinary Research Initiative • Jordan University College, Morogoro

A research group where you learn data analysis and mathematical modelling — working on real problems that matter for JUCo and for public health in Tanzania.

Cohort Size: 10–15 researchers
Sessions: Weekly • 2 hours
Duration: 12-month programme
Tools: R (free & open-source)

What Will You Gain?

💻 Hands-on coding in R (free)
📊 Real data analysis & modelling skills
👥 Peer network & mentorship
🗣️ Presentation skills
⭐ Individual research topic for your degree
🎓 Better prepared for postgraduate study
🌐 Foundation for data-driven careers
🏅 Certificate of Research Participation

👤 Who Can Apply?

  • Mathematics or Computer Science students — or a Postgraduate Research Scientist
  • At least 2 academic years remaining (undergrad)
  • Currently enrolled (postgraduate)
  • No prior coding experience needed

📝 How To Apply

  1. Pick up the application form from the Department Office.
  2. Submit to Dr. Anna Fome at anna.fome@juco.ac.tz.
