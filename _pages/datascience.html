JUCo Data Science & Modelling Research Group

An interdisciplinary research initiative · Jordan University College, Morogoro

A research group where you learn data analysis and mathematical modelling while working on real-world problems in education, health, and development.

What You Will Gain

Hands-on Coding in R

Practical programming experience using R (free & open-source).

Real Data Analysis

Work with real datasets and build applied modelling skills.

Research Experience

Develop and contribute to individual and group research projects.

Presentation Skills

Learn how to present and communicate research findings effectively.

Career Foundation

Build a strong base for data-driven careers and postgraduate study.

Certificate of Participation

Receive recognition for active research participation.

Who Can Apply?

  • Mathematics or Computer Science students
  • Postgraduate research students or early-career researchers
  • Students with at least 2 academic years remaining (undergraduate)
  • Currently enrolled postgraduate students
  • No prior coding experience required

How to Apply

  1. Pick up the application form from the Department Office
  2. Submit to Dr. Anna Fome
  3. Email: anna.fome@juco.ac.tz
