JUCo Data Science & Modelling Research Group

An Interdisciplinary Early-Career Research Initiative · Jordan University College, Morogoro

A structured research environment where early-career researchers develop skills in data science, statistical analysis, and mathematical modelling by working on real institutional and health-related problems in Tanzania.

Apply Now Explore Programme

Programme Overview

The JUCo DSG is a 12-month pilot research programme designed to bridge the gap between classroom learning and applied research. Participants work in small interdisciplinary teams to analyse data, build models, and develop research projects grounded in JUCo institutional systems and real-world health and development challenges.

Training Curriculum (2026/2027 Cohort)

Month 1–4: R Programming & Data Analysis Foundations
Month 5–7: Mathematical Modelling & Differential Equations
Month 8–11: Individual Research Project Development
Month 11: Internal Scientific Seminar
Month 12: Transition to Formal Research Projects

What You Will Gain

Statistical & Mathematical Modelling
