JUCo Data Science & Modelling Research Group Applications Open

Calling All Female Minds in Mathematics & Computer Science at JUCo!

The JUCo Data Science & Modelling Research Group (JUCo DSRG) is officially accepting applications for its first 12-month pilot cohort.

As a female scientist, I know how intimidating it can feel to step into data science. That is why this program requires ZERO prior coding experience. We will learn R Programming and statistical modelling together — step-by-step, from scratch.

If you want to solve real-world Tanzanian health and institutional problems using data, I strongly encourage you to apply. Let’s build the next generation of female data scientists at JUCo together.

JUCo DSRG is committed to closing the gender gap in quantitative sciences. Female students and applicants from underrepresented backgrounds are strongly encouraged to apply.
